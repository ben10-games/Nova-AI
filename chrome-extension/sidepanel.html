Settings

In use: —

Page awareness

Nova reads the tab you're looking at only on sites you allowed, and sends it to your model only with the message you send.

Without this, Nova can see a tab right after you click the Nova icon on it. Turning it on asks Chrome for access to all sites — which also lets Nova read every page you visit (still only sent to your own model with your messages; actions still need your approval).

Chats

Off by default. Saved chats stay in this browser only (never sent anywhere); page text and screenshots are not saved. Open them with 🕘.

    Hands-off mode 🙌

    Also the 🙌 button under the message box. Nova clicks, types, searches and opens pages on its own and keeps going until everything you asked for is done ("search these 3 things and open each one", "fill in both pages of this form with …"). It still stops and asks before anything that submits, sends, buys, deletes or goes to the next question, before opening a site it can't read yet, and for anything outside the browser. ■ Stop always works.

    E.g. "fill this out and submit it": Nova submits without asking. It still asks before buying, paying or deleting anything, and on school homework and quiz sites (DeltaMath, IXL, Khan Academy, Google Forms, …). Check what it did afterwards: a small model can misread a page.

    Auto-allowed sites

    On these sites Nova clicks, types and opens pages of the same site without asking each time (until Chrome closes). Submitting, sending, buying, deleting, other sites and searches always ask.
