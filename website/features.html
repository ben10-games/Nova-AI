Features

Everything Syntaz can do

Syntaz lives in Chrome's side panel, right next to the website you're on.

Knows the page you're on

No copy and paste. Just ask "what does this page say?", "explain this" or "summarize this". Syntaz also shows suggestions that fit the page.

Does things on websites for you

Syntaz can click, type, fill in forms, pick from lists, scroll, and search the web or a site like YouTube. Say "open remix beatbox on YouTube" and it opens the video. A violet Syntaz cursor shows on the page while it works.

Asks first, or goes hands-off

Normally every click or keystroke needs your OK. Turn on hands-off mode and Syntaz does a whole task without asking each step. It still always asks before submitting, sending, buying or deleting.

Do it for me

One tap and Syntaz does what the page needs, for example answering a question. It fills things in, but it never submits by itself. You check it and press the button.

Q3. What is 15% of 80? 12

Sees pictures and graphs

Ask about a chart, a diagram or a photo on the page. This works when the AI model you use can see images.

which month was the highest?

"Hears" videos

Syntaz reads a YouTube video's transcript, so you can ask "what did they just say?" It can't hear music.

…and that's why the sky looks blue.

Shortcuts

Type /summarize, /explain or /translate. Press Alt+N to open Syntaz, or right-click anything and choose "Ask Syntaz about this".

/summarize /explain /translate

The Syntaz window

Find Syntaz on your desktop and in the Start menu or Applications. Start or stop it, change the AI, update with one click, or uninstall.
