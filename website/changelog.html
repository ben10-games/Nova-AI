Changelog

What's new

Every Syntaz version. The Syntaz window shows Update when a new one is out.

1.3 Latest

New name, new logo

  • Nova AI is now Syntaz AI.
  • A new logo: a white S with a small spark, on the same violet-to-pink square.
  • Older Nova installs update to Syntaz by themselves, and keep your key and chats.

1.2

A new look, and easier to use

  • One look everywhere, with a light theme that follows your computer.
  • Suggestions that fit the page you're on, like "Summarize this video" on YouTube.
  • Steps in plain words, and clearer Allow and Deny buttons.
  • The setup window checks your key as soon as you paste it.

1.1

Syntaz Lite

  • Syntaz in one file: double-click it, paste a free key, and chat. Nothing to install.
  • Add pictures and text files to your questions.

1.0

First release

  • Syntaz in Chrome's side panel: it reads the page and does things on websites, with your approval.
  • Hands-off mode, "Do it for me", and reading YouTube transcripts.
  • Free Google Gemini, or a model on your own computer with LM Studio.
  • Separate downloads for Windows and Mac, set up from one window.
