Safety & privacy

You stay in charge

Syntaz asks before it clicks anything. Here's exactly what it will and won't do, and where your data goes.

Syntaz will

  • Ask for your OK before every click or keystroke.
  • Always ask before submitting, sending, buying or deleting, even in hands-off mode.
  • Show a violet Syntaz cursor so you can see what it's doing.
  • Treat web pages as untrusted, so a page can't boss it around.

Syntaz won't

  • Read password or payment fields.
  • Solve CAPTCHAs or get past logins.
  • Submit, buy or delete anything unless you tell it to.
  • Hand in work by itself. "Do it for me" fills things in, you press the button.

Where your data goes

With Gemini

Your messages and the page you ask about go to Google's AI to get an answer.

With LM Studio

The AI runs on your own computer. Nothing leaves it.

Your key

Your Gemini key stays on your computer. On this website's chat, it stays in your browser.

Stop, Pause, Disconnect

These always work, any time.

StopEnds the task right now. Syntaz stops clicking and typing.
PauseFreezes the task so you can look. Continue when you're ready.
DisconnectTurn Syntaz off in the Syntaz window. It can't do anything until you start it again.
