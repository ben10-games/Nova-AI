FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Short answers to the things people ask most.

Is it free?

Yes. Syntaz and Syntaz Lite are free. The Gemini key from Google is free too.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. With free Google Gemini, Syntaz works even on slow computers. Only LM Studio (running the AI on your own computer) needs a strong graphics card.

What's the daily limit?

Gemini's free tier allows a limited number of requests a day, roughly 100 tasks with the default model. It resets every day.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me?

Syntaz doesn't come from an app store, so your computer double-checks. On Windows, click More info → Run anyway. On Mac, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Can it do my homework for me?

It explains things and helps you work through them. You stay in charge, and it doesn't hand anything in by itself.

How do I update?

Open the Syntaz window and click Update. One click and you have the newest version.

How do I uninstall?

Open the Syntaz window and click Uninstall.

Does it work on Chromebooks?

Syntaz Lite does. It's one file that opens in any browser, with nothing to install. Get Syntaz Lite
