Set it up

  1. 1Unzip Syntaz-for-Windows.zip.
  2. 2Double-click Syntaz Setup (Windows).bat. The Syntaz window opens.
  3. 3Click Set up Syntaz.
  4. 4Pick your AI and paste its key: free Gemini (how to get one), or your own OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or xAI (Grok) key (you pay them). Then click Install.
  5. 5Add Syntaz to Chrome: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the chrome-extension folder.
  6. 6Done! Press Alt+N on any website to open Syntaz.
Windows warning? If SmartScreen warns you, click More info → Run anyway. This happens because Syntaz is new and made by one developer.

About 2 minutes

Get your free Gemini key

The key lets Syntaz use Google's free AI. It stays on your computer.

Open Google AI Studio
  1. 1Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. 2Click Create API key.
  3. 3Copy the key and paste it into Syntaz.
